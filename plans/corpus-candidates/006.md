### The load-bearing moments

**The self-comparison bug of 005 is solved deliberately** — `removeAt(bookings, 0)`
excludes the head before scanning. But the scan only ever compares *the head*
against the tail, so a conflict between elements 2 and 3 is invisible, and
`hasConflicts` inherits that.

**Real time is lost to an incomplete reference.** *"So `drop` is NOT explicitly
listed in the prelude section. I must implement it or avoid it."* — `drop` is a
native, and prompt v4 did not list the natives. This is the gap v5 closes.

**Tuple syntax is invented and correct.** `Maybe<(Booking, Booking)>` and
`Some((b1, x))` appear nowhere in the prompt; `ast.rs` does have `Type::Tuple`,
`Expr::Tuple` and `Pattern::Tuple`. The model refuses unlisted *functions* but
freely extrapolates *syntax*.
