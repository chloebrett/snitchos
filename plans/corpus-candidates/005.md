### The load-bearing moment

The model **finds the self-comparison bug and argues itself out of it**:

> *"x = b1. `overlaps(b1, b1)` -> True. So checkOverlaps returns true even if
> there's no real conflict between distinct bookings…"*

then, a few lines later:

> *"Wait, if I have `[b1, b1]`, do they conflict? Yes. So checking all pairs is
> fine for 'exclusive use'."*

It conflates *two identical bookings in a list conflict* (true) with *a booking
compared against itself conflicts* (the bug), and ships. More reasoning made the
program worse.

Separately, `cooldown` is abandoned on discovering the prelude has `max(xs)` but
no `max(a, b)` — the soft-constraint phrasing working as intended.
