missed. A better problem model dissolved both bug classes rather than dodging
them.

### The load-bearing moments

**The prelude settled a rule.** The model worried that "booleans are the words
`and`/`or`/`not`" might forbid the literals `true`/`false`, checked the prelude,
found `fold(xs, false, …)`, and correctly concluded the rule was about operators.
This is exactly what embedding `prelude.st` verbatim was for — an authority the
model can consult rather than guess against.

**`use Maybe` was considered and rejected** as built-in. Candidate 008 (9B) got
this wrong and imported it.

**Comments were revised toward "why" on purpose**, with the model quoting its own
rejected "what"-style comments before replacing them.

**The cost is the problem.** Roughly nine minutes, and a large share of it is the
constraint re-verification cycle above — repeated passes that change nothing.
