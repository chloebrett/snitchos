### The load-bearing moments

**The `ext prod` gap is derived step by step.** The trace records
`prod Name(fields...)` and `ext name(params) -> Type = body` as two separate
rules, finds no rule for *exporting a type*, and composes them into
`ext Room(...) = prod Room(...)` — three times.

**`&&` is used while the rule is being quoted.** *"Reference: bool? No, it says
'Booleans are words, not symbols'. `a and b`."* — and every conditional in the
output uses `&&` anyway. Neither exemplar in prompt v1 contains an `and` or `or`,
so the rule had no code-shaped backup.

**`fold` is never resolved.** The reference's piped `xs |> fold(0, f)` and the
exemplar's `fold(1..=n, "", f)` read as a contradiction; the shipped
`fold([], [], (acc, b) -> …)` is a fusion of both.
